About NRC

The North Redstone community was not a full thought idea on how the building would run. The game is a version 1 of County Jail Roleplay. County Jail Roleplay was removed from Roblox due to a false DMCA claim. North Redstone is not an exact copy — it is a reference to version one of the game.
